Serve the platform skill family from the core UI build — `node_modules/{everything-dev,every-plugin,better-near-auth}/skills` copy into `dist/skills/<package>/` and are reachable at `/skills/<package>/<skill>/SKILL.md` (with the rest of the family listed from `/skill.md`). Children get the same copies from the published npm tarballs.
