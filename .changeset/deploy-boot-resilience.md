Deploy boot resilience: the orchestrator's remote boot fetches (host pin resolve, manifest fetch, module load) retry transient undici socket failures with a bounded schedule instead of dying on the first `fetch failed` blip (the 2026-11-20 rc.46 train lost its smoke gate to one). The post-publish smoke fails fast when the container logs a `Failed to start:` line instead of burning the full 240s probe deadline, and surfaces undici's hidden `error.cause` in poller failures. The publish gas-prepayment check reads the raw yoctoNEAR wire balance (`rpc.getAccount`) instead of near-kit's lossy human-NEAR `getBalance` string — the prepayment-lock protection (the 2026-11-04 outage guard) has been silently soft-skipping since the near-kit migration, and now actually verifies the account can afford the gas lock again; a non-integer RPC response classifies as an unexpected format instead of misreporting the RPC as unreachable, and the custody balance gates share the same yocto parsing — an unreadable balance soft-skips preflight with a named reason and fails loud on the hard gate.
